# Creating an account

## Authorization

To create and log in to your accounts, click on the "Sign In" button in the top right corner of the page. You will be redirected to the login page where you will be prompted to continue with your Google account. We've opted to use Google sign-in, as most schools provide a Google Account to their students, or you can create a new Google Account for free. This way, you don't have to remember another password, and authorization is quick and easy.

## Onboarding

After you've signed in, you will be redirected to the onboarding page. Here, you will be prompted to enter your first and last name, as well as your grade and school. This information is used to provide school-specific content and to help us better understand our users, such as schedules, periods, and events. If your school is not listed, feel free to start one by clicking the "Create School" button, please refer to the [Creating a School](/help/getting-started/managing-schools) page for more information and assistance.

After entering school information, you will continue to connecting your Canvas account with Catalyst. There will be an animated visual guide to help you through the process. If you have any questions or need assistance, please refer to the [Canvas Integration](/help/troubleshooting/canvas-needs-attention) page.

After accessing your Canvas courses, you will be prompted to re-create your schedule by selecting the courses you are enrolled in with the corresponding periods. This information is used to provide a personalized experience, such as viewing your schedule, assignments, and grades.

### Changing Information

After signing up, you are always able to change these settings that you've entered during the onboarding process. To do so, click on the "Settings" button in the top right corner of the page.

### Continuing Onward

After completing the onboarding process, you will be redirected to the home page. Here, you will see your schedule, assignments, and grades. To see more about the dashboard, please refer to our [Navigating the dashboard](/help/getting-started/navigating-the-dashboard) page.
