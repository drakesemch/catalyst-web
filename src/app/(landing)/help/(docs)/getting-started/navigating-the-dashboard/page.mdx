# Navigating the Dashboard

## Overview

The dashboard is the first page you see when you log in to Catalyst. At the top of the page, you will have a tabbed navigation bar, which contains links to different sections of the app, such as our homepage, courses, social, tools, inbox, and profile. Each of sections will allow you navigate to each part of our application with ease. On mobile, this navigation is at the bottom. This contains a home tab, course, tools, and profile, which will contain buttons for social, inbox, notifications, and more.

Below the navigation bar, and the gretting message, you will prompted with cards, showing your current class, to quickly navigate to that course, a mini schedule, with a countdown timer, a button for assignment list, and inbox. These cards are dynamic to show you the latest information, and will change based on your current status.

Below the cards, you are provided with your todo list. This is very similar to the Canvas todo list, with improvements. You can easily create new tasks, mark them as complete, and show you your remaining time on tasks. There are also helpful links to assignments presented in the todo list.

## Navigation

The courses tab has been redesigned to show you a list of your current courses, and a search bar to find new courses. These courses are highlighted when active, shows you your missing assignments, and shows you grade, so no more fumbling around Canvas' UI to find the course and your grade.

The social tab, located under the profile section on mobile, is a feature not presented before in Canvas, and is a way to connect with your classmates. You can add friends, create chats and groups, and share your work with others. This is a great way to collaborate with others, and get help on assignments. You can learn more about managing friends and groups [here](/help/getting-started/managing-social).

The tools tab is another new addition, this is a place to find tools, that you may have to leave Canvas to use, these consist of a calculator, periodic table, grade calculators, and much more.

The inbox tab, also located under the profile section on mobile, is a place for all of your Canvas messages. THis is where you can message teachers and students who are not yet on the Catalyst platform, which they should be :).

In the profile tab, you can view your profile, edit settings, view and manage school, access notifications, and more. This is your hub for most things Catalyst.
