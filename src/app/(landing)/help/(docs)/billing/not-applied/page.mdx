# Subscription not applied

## Why is my subscription not applied?

If you have a subscription and it is not applied, it could be due to a few reasons:

1. **Payment Failure**: If your payment method has failed, your subscription will not be applied. You can check your payment method in your account settings.
2. **Account Issue**: If there is an issue with your account, your subscription may not be applied. You can check your account settings to see if there are any issues.
3. **Technical Issue**: Sometimes there may be a technical issue that prevents your subscription from being applied. In this case, you can contact support for assistance.

If you are having trouble with your subscription, please contact support for help.
